# Video presentation script (about two minutes)

“Hello, I am Siddharth [full name], roll number [roll number], from CSE Panthers, Semester 3. My project is the Employee Payroll and Statutory Deduction Management System, guided by Dr. Sahithi, for course 25TU03MJM0 Database Management Systems.

Organizations need consistent employee, salary, attendance, deduction, loan and payment records. Spreadsheet formulas can be difficult to audit. This project uses a MySQL relational database and a Python desktop interface.

The database separates employees, departments, designations, salary structures, payroll periods, attendance, loans, earnings and deductions. Payroll Details resolves the employee-period relationship, and a unique constraint ensures one payroll per employee per period. The design is normalized to 3NF to reduce duplicated facts.

Here I search for an employee and open the payroll summary. The reports show gross and net pay, deductions, department cost, loan balances and bank transfer status. Payroll calculation runs in a transaction so its payroll lines and loan repayment update together.

The displayed pension and tax percentages are fictional demonstration rules, not official statutory rates, and the bank transfer is a record rather than a live bank connection. Thank you.”
